BermudaThings

Twenty-one miles long. Most visitors see one beach.

Bermuda is 21 miles long, and most visitors see the pier, one beach and a bus. BermudaThings covers the whole island — Horseshoe Bay and the south shore beaches, the forts, Crystal Caves, St George's and the Dockyard.

Ferries and buses, properly explained

Bermuda has no rental cars. Getting around means the ferry and the bus, and the app explains the passes, the routes and which one actually gets you where you want to go.

Off the cruise pier

Every place shows minutes from where your ship ties up, with an all-aboard reminder so you make the last ferry back.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time — it all works in airplane mode.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
