Twenty-one miles long. Most visitors see one beach.

Now part of Port Things. The Bermuda guide lives on inside Port Things, one offline app for cruise ports and trips.

See the Bermuda guide on portthings.com

Bermuda is 21 miles long, and most visitors see the pier, one beach and a bus. BermudaThings covers the whole island — Horseshoe Bay and the south shore beaches, the forts, Crystal Caves, St George's and the Dockyard.

Ferries and buses, properly explained

Bermuda has no rental cars. Getting around means the ferry and the bus, and the app explains the passes, the routes and which one actually gets you where you want to go.

Off the cruise pier

Every place near the Dockyard, Hamilton and St. George's piers shows walking minutes from the ship, twenty ready-made days carry an all-aboard reminder, and a Back-to-Ship compass works with no signal.

Works offline, for real

Every place, description and opening time ships inside the app, and a free Offline Port Pack saves the photos before you sail, so the guide works in airplane mode. Only the map background needs a signal.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
