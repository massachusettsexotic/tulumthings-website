Iceland rewards people who know where to look.

The whole island in 23 categories — waterfalls, glaciers, volcanoes, black beaches, puffin cliffs, museums, campsites — hand-written descriptions for the famous stops, OpenStreetMap for the rest, with live "open now" hours.

Hot springs vs town pools

Iceland's secret is that "hot spring" means two different things: wild springs and lagoons out in the landscape, and the beloved municipal pools in every town. IcelandThings is the only guide that treats them as the two categories they really are.

Built for the Ring Road

Everything is organized so a day of driving becomes a day of stops — with honest tips about which "attractions" are gift shops. Iceland's tour operators (many on Bókun) are linked directly, never resold.

Works without a connection on Iceland's empty stretches. Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
