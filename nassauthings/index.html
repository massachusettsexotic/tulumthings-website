NassauThings

Your ship docks downtown. The island is bigger than Bay Street.

Every cruise ship ties up at Prince George Wharf, right downtown. NassauThings covers what is past the straw market: the three forts and the Queen's Staircase, Cable Beach, Paradise Island, the Fish Fry at Arawak Cay, and the boat days out to Blue Lagoon Island and the Exuma cays.

Jitneys, taxis and water taxis, explained

Which jitney goes to Cable Beach, what a taxi should cost to each zone, and when the water taxi to Paradise Island is the better ride. Plain, honest safety notes where you need them.

Off the cruise pier

Every place shows minutes from the pier, with an all-aboard reminder and twenty-three ready-made days that get you back to the ship on time.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time, so the guide works in airplane mode. Only the map background needs a signal.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
