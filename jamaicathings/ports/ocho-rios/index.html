Ocho Rios cruise port guide

Jamaica · from JamaicaThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Ocho Rios Cruise Ship Pier

The main Ocho Rios berth is right in town: Island Village, Turtle Beach and the craft market are a few minutes' walk. Dunn's River is 10–15 minutes by licensed taxi. Some ships use Reynolds Pier just west instead — check your ship's port sheet.

Typically 8 AM – 5 PM · Jamaica keeps Eastern Standard Time all year — check whether your ship keeps a different time

Reynolds Pier, Ocho Rios

Reynolds Pier is a long industrial pier a few hundred metres west of the main Ocho Rios berth; allow 10 minutes to walk its length. Dunn's River and Mystic Mountain are 10 minutes west by licensed taxi.

Typically 8 AM – 5 PM · Jamaica keeps Eastern Standard Time all year — check whether your ship keeps a different time

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Island Village

    7 min walk · Shopping & Souvenirs

    The shopping and entertainment centre beside the Ocho Rios cruise pier: shops, a Margaritaville, cafés and a small beach, right at the pier gate.

  2. Turtle Beach

    8 min walk · Beaches

    The long public beach along Ocho Rios Bay (officially Ocho Rios Bay Beach), minutes from the cruise pier, with a view of the ships.

  3. Ocho Rios Craft Market

    11 min walk · Craft Markets

    The town craft market with stalls of carvings, straw bags, jewellery and T-shirts, a short walk from the pier.

  4. Turtle River Park

    12 min walk · Parks & Gardens

    A small, landscaped town park with fountains, ponds and a river walk in the middle of Ocho Rios, free and shady.

  5. Miss T's Kitchen

    16 min walk · Restaurants

    A colourful, much-loved Jamaican restaurant on Main Street in Ocho Rios serving oxtail, curry goat, ackee and saltfish and jerk in a garden setting.

  6. Fern Gully

    20 min walk · Parks & Gardens

    A road through a former riverbed south of Ocho Rios, roofed by tree ferns and forest for about three miles — a cool, green drive on the way to the hills.

  7. Konoko Falls & Park

    26 min walk · Waterfalls & River Rafting

    Gardens, a cascading waterfall, a small zoo and a museum of Taíno history on the old Shaw Park estate above Ocho Rios, with views over the bay.

  8. Mystic Mountain

    31 min walk · Family Fun

    A rainforest adventure park on the hill above Dunn's River: a chairlift (the Sky Explorer) up through the canopy, a Jamaican-bobsled roller coaster, ziplines and an infinity pool with views of the bay.

  9. Dolphin Cove Ocho Rios

    46 min walk · Family Fun

    A marine park next to Dunn's River with dolphin and stingray encounters in a cove, plus a jungle trail.

  10. Dunn's River Falls & Park

    50 min walk · Waterfalls & River Rafting

    Jamaica's most famous waterfall: about 180 metres (600 ft) of travertine terraces that you climb from the beach to the top, holding hands in a human chain behind a guide.

  11. Prospect Plantation

    4 mi / 7 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Tours & Adventure

    A working estate east of Ocho Rios with jitney tours through the plantation and camel and horse rides — the adventure company Chukka runs it today.

  12. Scotchies Drax Hall

    6 mi / 9 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Restaurants

    The Ocho Rios branch of the Scotchies jerk centre, on the main road west of town at Drax Hall: jerk pork, chicken and sausage from the pimento pit.

  13. Island Gully Falls (Blue Hole)

    7 mi / 11 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Waterfalls & River Rafting

    A chain of limestone pools and cascades in the hills above Ocho Rios, better known as the Blue Hole or Secret Falls: jump, swing on the rope and swim in cold blue water.

  14. James Bond Beach

    14 mi / 22 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Beaches

    A beach on a small peninsula at Oracabessa, near Ian Fleming's GoldenEye estate where the Bond novels were written.

  15. Bob Marley Mausoleum

    17 mi / 28 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Museums & Culture

    Bob Marley's birthplace and resting place in the village of Nine Mile, high in the St. Ann hills: the house he grew up in, the 'rock pillow' of his song, and the mausoleum where he lies.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Agree the fare, then get in
Say where you're going and ask: 'How much, per person or for the car, US or Jamaican?' Agree before the door shuts, and pay at the end. For a round trip, agree the pickup time and pay when you're back at the port.
Set an alarm on ship time
Jamaica doesn't change its clocks. Ships often keep a different time. Check the time on the ship's app or a clock on board and set your phone alarm by that — and give yourself an hour's margin for traffic on the coast road.
Beat the tour buses
Dunn's River, Mystic Mountain and the Blue Hole fill up between 10 AM and 1 PM on ship days. Be first off the ship and first at the gate, or go after 1 PM when the tours head back.
