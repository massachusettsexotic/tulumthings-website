Montego Bay cruise port guide

Jamaica · from JamaicaThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Montego Bay Cruise Port (Freeport)

Freeport is not walkable into town: take a licensed taxi (red PP plates) from the dispatch at the terminal — Doctor's Cave and the Hip Strip are about 10 minutes, Rose Hall 25. Agree the fare first, and allow extra time in the afternoon traffic back to the port.

Typically 8 AM – 5 PM · Jamaica keeps Eastern Standard Time all year — check whether your ship keeps a different time

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Harbour Street Craft Market

    41 min walk · Craft Markets

    Montego Bay's biggest craft market, rows of stalls selling carvings, straw work, T-shirts and paintings along Harbour Street downtown.

  2. Walter Fletcher Beach

    43 min walk · Beaches

    The public beach and small park on the bay at the downtown end of the Hip Strip, with calm water and a view of the ships across the harbour.

  3. Sam Sharpe Square

    44 min walk · Great Houses & Landmarks

    The heart of downtown Montego Bay, named for the Baptist deacon who led the 1831 Christmas Rebellion of enslaved people and was hanged here in 1832; he is a National Hero.

  4. St. James Parish Church

    44 min walk · Great Houses & Landmarks

    A white Georgian church of the 1770s on Church Street, rebuilt after the 1957 earthquake, with marble memorials to the planter families of the parish.

  5. Pork Pit

    45 min walk · Restaurants

    A long-running open-air jerk joint at the airport end of the Hip Strip: jerk pork, chicken and ribs smoked over pimento wood, with festival and roast breadfruit.

  6. The Hip Strip (Jimmy Cliff Boulevard)

    48 min walk · Great Houses & Landmarks

    Montego Bay's seafront strip of bars, jerk stands, souvenir stalls and beach clubs, renamed Jimmy Cliff Boulevard in honour of the reggae star from Somerton, St. James.

  7. Margaritaville Montego Bay

    50 min walk · Bars & Nightlife

    The multi-level open-air bar and restaurant on the Hip Strip with a waterslide into the sea, a sundeck and trampolines on the water.

  8. Doctor's Cave Beach

    58 min walk · Beaches

    Montego Bay's most famous beach: a curve of fine sand and clear, calm water on the Hip Strip, run as a bathing club since the early 1900s and made famous in the 1920s when an English doctor praised its water as healing.

  9. Dead End Beach

    4 mi / 6 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Beaches

    A free public beach at the end of Kent Avenue beside the airport runway — locals' sand, planes landing overhead, and one of the best sunset spots in Montego Bay.

  10. Scotchies Montego Bay

    9 mi / 14 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Restaurants

    The original Scotchies jerk centre on the Falmouth road at Coral Gardens: jerk pork and chicken cooked under zinc sheets on pimento logs, with festival, bammy and roast yam.

  11. White Witch Golf Course

    11 mi / 18 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Golf & Spas

    The Rose Hall resort's hilltop championship course, with sea views from most holes.

  12. Rose Hall Great House

    12 mi / 19 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Great Houses & Landmarks

    The restored 1770s Georgian mansion on the hill above Rose Hall, the most famous great house in Jamaica, wrapped in the legend of Annie Palmer, the 'White Witch of Rose Hall'.

  13. Greenwood Great House

    17 mi / 28 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Great Houses & Landmarks

    The Barrett family's 1800 great house on a ridge above the sea east of Rose Hall, full of original furniture, antique musical instruments and the family library — the most authentic of the great-house interiors.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Agree the fare, then get in
Say where you're going and ask: 'How much, per person or for the car, US or Jamaican?' Agree before the door shuts, and pay at the end. For a round trip, agree the pickup time and pay when you're back at the port.
Set an alarm on ship time
Jamaica doesn't change its clocks. Ships often keep a different time. Check the time on the ship's app or a clock on board and set your phone alarm by that — and give yourself an hour's margin for traffic on the coast road.
Beat the tour buses
Dunn's River, Mystic Mountain and the Blue Hole fill up between 10 AM and 1 PM on ship days. Be first off the ship and first at the gate, or go after 1 PM when the tours head back.
