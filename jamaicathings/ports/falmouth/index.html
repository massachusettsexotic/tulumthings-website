Where ships dock

Historic Falmouth Cruise Port

Historic Falmouth is on foot from the port gate — Water Square is about 10 minutes. Licensed taxis and tours wait in the port's transport area for Martha Brae (20 minutes) and Rose Hall (30 minutes). Parts of town are still being repaired after Hurricane Melissa (October 2025).

Typically 8 AM – 5 PM · Jamaica keeps Eastern Standard Time all year — check whether your ship keeps a different time

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. The Artisan Village at Falmouth

    9 min walk · Craft Markets

    A crafts village opened in 2024 beside the cruise port, with Jamaican makers selling carvings, paintings, jewellery and textiles, plus food stalls.

  2. Water Square

    10 min walk · Great Houses & Landmarks

    Falmouth's central square, laid out in the 1790s around a reservoir that — as Falmouth likes to say — gave the town piped water before New York City had it.

  3. Albert George Market

    10 min walk · Craft Markets

    The 1894 market hall on Water Square, a cut-stone Georgian-style building named for two of Queen Victoria's grandsons; craft and produce stalls inside.

  4. William Knibb Memorial Baptist Church

    13 min walk · Great Houses & Landmarks

    The Baptist church named for William Knibb, the English missionary who campaigned against slavery; on Emancipation night, 1 August 1838, his congregation buried a chain, whip and iron collar here.

  5. St. Peter's Anglican Church

    17 min walk · Great Houses & Landmarks

    Falmouth's parish church of 1795, a broad Georgian church in a walled churchyard at the west end of town, one of the oldest buildings in Trelawny.

  6. Glistening Waters (Luminous Lagoon)

    43 min walk · Boat Trips & Lagoon Tours

    The Luminous Lagoon at Rock, just east of Falmouth, where the Martha Brae meets the sea and microscopic plankton glow blue-green when the water is stirred.

  7. Rafting on the Martha Brae

    4 mi / 6 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Waterfalls & River Rafting

    Bamboo rafts poled by licensed raft captains for about 75 minutes down a quiet three-mile stretch of the Martha Brae River, through bamboo and forest.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Agree the fare, then get in
Say where you're going and ask: 'How much, per person or for the car, US or Jamaican?' Agree before the door shuts, and pay at the end. For a round trip, agree the pickup time and pay when you're back at the port.
Set an alarm on ship time
Jamaica doesn't change its clocks. Ships often keep a different time. Check the time on the ship's app or a clock on board and set your phone alarm by that — and give yourself an hour's margin for traffic on the coast road.
Beat the tour buses
Dunn's River, Mystic Mountain and the Blue Hole fill up between 10 AM and 1 PM on ship days. Be first off the ship and first at the gate, or go after 1 PM when the tours head back.
