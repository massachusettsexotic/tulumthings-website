CaboThings

Twenty beaches. Only some of them are safe to swim.

Los Cabos is two towns, twenty beaches and a lot of people trying to sell you something. CaboThings covers Cabo San Lucas, the Tourist Corridor and San José del Cabo the way a local friend would.

Which beaches you can actually swim at

The Pacific side has a current that kills people. Every beach in the app is rated for swimming honestly, not by how it photographs.

Boats and whales, without the hard sell

Whale watching, the Arch, snorkel trips — real operators with their own booking pages, so you pay their price rather than a timeshare tout's.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time — it all works in airplane mode.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
