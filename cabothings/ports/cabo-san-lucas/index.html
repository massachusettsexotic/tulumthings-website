Cabo San Lucas cruise port guide

Baja California Sur, Mexico · from CaboThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Cabo San Lucas tender pier (marina)

Cabo is a tender port: ships anchor in the bay and tender boats bring you to this pier in the marina. The boardwalk, downtown and the water taxis to the Arch are all on foot from here. Allow extra time for the tender line on the way back — the last tender leaves before all-aboard.

Usually a morning-to-afternoon call · the last-tender time is in your ship's daily program

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Water Taxis to Lover's Beach

    1 min walk · Boats, Fishing & Whales

    The panga fleet that runs the marina–Arch–Lover's Beach loop all day. The essential Cabo experience for about the price of a cocktail.

  2. Whale Watching (Cabo Marina)

    4 min walk · Boats, Fishing & Whales

    Humpback whales fill the bay from mid December to mid April — breaching, tail-slapping, mothers with calves — and small-boat tours from the marina put you almost among them.

  3. Sportfishing Fleet

    9 min walk · Boats, Fishing & Whales

    Cabo calls itself the marlin capital of the world, and the fleet that lines the marina is why — striped marlin year-round, blue and black marlin in late summer and fall, dorado, tuna and wahoo.

  4. Cabo Wabo Cantina

    10 min walk · Bars & Nightlife

    Sammy Hagar's cantina, the downtown institution since 1990: live rock bands nightly, tequila by the flight, and a crowd that ranges from spring breakers to retirees who saw Van Halen.

  5. Museo de Historia Natural de Cabo San Lucas

    12 min walk · Museums & Galleries

    A small, earnest natural-history museum on the plaza downtown — fossils, the whales, local geology, and the story of the peninsula — in a historic school building.

  6. Puerto Paraíso Mall

    12 min walk · Shopping & Souvenirs

    The air-conditioned mall on the marina: Cinemex cinema, a bowling alley, Luxury Avenue's designer boutiques next door, chain restaurants and clean restrooms — the town's refuge from a hot or rainy afternoon.

  7. El Squid Roe

    13 min walk · Bars & Nightlife

    Three floors of controlled chaos on Lázaro Cárdenas: waiters dancing on tables, shot girls, and a nightly party that peaks around 1 AM.

  8. Playa Solmar

    13 min walk · Beaches

    The long Pacific beach on the back of Land's End, below the Solmar and Grand Solmar resorts — wide, dramatic, and strictly for walking: the surf is enormous and the drop-off steep.

  9. Mango Deck

    14 min walk · Bars & Nightlife

    The loudest beach club on Médano: bikini contests, MC on a microphone, buckets of beer, spring-break energy all year.

  10. The Office on the Beach

    19 min walk · Restaurants

    The original Médano beach restaurant since 1979 — tables in the sand, a view straight at the Arch, fish tacos and margaritas, and a Mexican fiesta night on Thursdays.

  11. Mercado Mexicano

    20 min walk · Markets & Art Walk

    The downtown handicrafts market on Hidalgo: blankets, silver, pottery, tequila and a hundred t-shirts, in a warren of small stalls where bargaining is expected.

  12. Divorce Beach

    21 min walk · Beaches

    The Pacific face of Lover's Beach, a hundred steps through the rocks: huge, empty, and beautiful, with a shore break that kills swimmers every year.

  13. El Arco (The Arch)

    26 min walk · Landmarks & Nature

    The granite arch at the very tip of the Baja peninsula, where the Sea of Cortez meets the Pacific — the symbol of Cabo, reachable only by boat.

  14. Pelican Rock

    27 min walk · Snorkel & Dive

    The rock just off Lover's Beach with the best easy snorkeling in Cabo San Lucas — sergeant majors, angelfish and the occasional sea turtle in a few metres of protected water.

  15. Lover's Beach

    27 min walk · Beaches

    The famous double-sided beach at Land's End: a calm swimmable cove on the Sea of Cortez side and, through a gap in the rocks, the wild Pacific side called Divorce Beach.

Getting around: what the app tells you

The 'free' breakfast is a 3-hour sales pitch
Anyone at the airport, the marina or your hotel lobby offering free tours, cash, or a 'resort preview' in exchange for '90 minutes' is selling timeshares. The 90 minutes is three hours of high-pressure sales, and people do sign. 'No gracias' and keep walking; it's not rude, it's expected.
