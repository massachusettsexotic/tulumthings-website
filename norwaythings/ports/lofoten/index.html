Lofoten cruise port guide

Norway · from NorwayThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Svolvær harbour quay (Leknes for large ships; tenders on busy days)

Svolvær is at your feet. The Trollfjord boats leave from the harbour; Henningsvær is a 40-minute bus. Leknes ships are 20 minutes from the Viking museum.

Typically 8 AM – 5 PM

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Lofoten War Memorial Museum

    6 min walk · Museums & Culture

    A private museum in the centre of Svolvær with the largest collection of WWII uniforms and objects in Norway, on the 1941 commando raid and the German occupation of the islands.

  2. Trollfjord Boat Trip

    8 min walk · Fjord Cruises & Boat Trips

    The 2 km fjord so narrow — 100 m at the mouth — that the boats turn around under the cliffs, with sea eagles fed off the stern on the way.

  3. Magic Ice Svolvær

    9 min walk · Family Fun

    An ice bar and gallery in an old fish-freezing plant on the harbour: ice sculptures of Lofoten fishing life at minus 5°C, a drink from an ice glass, ponchos provided.

  4. Svolværgeita (The Svolvær Goat)

    28 min walk · Hikes, Glaciers & Rides

    The two-horned rock pinnacle 150 m above the town, where climbers jump between the horns; the marked path from town reaches the base of the Goat and the Fløya summit above it in 90 minutes with the best view of Svolvær.

  5. Henningsvær

    14 mi / 23 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    The 'Venice of Lofoten': a fishing village on a scatter of islets joined by bridges, 25 km from Svolvær, with the football pitch on the rocks that went viral, galleries, cafés and cod-drying racks.

  6. Lofotr Viking Museum

    27 mi / 44 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Museums & Culture

    The reconstructed 83-metre longhouse of a Viking chieftain at Borg on Vestvågøy — the largest ever found — with a feast hall, costumed staff, a Viking ship to row on the lake and a museum of the finds.

  7. Reine

    57 mi / 91 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    The fishing village at the foot of the Reinebringen peak on Moskenesøya, 130 km south-west of Svolvær — the photograph on every Lofoten poster.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Ship time, tender time, and the last boat back
All-aboard is in ship's time, which is not always local time — check the daily programme. In Geiranger the last tender back runs 30–60 minutes before all-aboard and the queue is longest right then; in Flåm the train and the fjord boat both run late on two-ship days. Be at the gangway 30 minutes early. The ship waits for its own excursions, not for you.
Book the operators direct, not the ship's desk
The Flåm Railway, Nærøyfjord boat, Loen Skylift, Fløibanen, Lysefjord and Geiranger cruises all sell online at fixed prices; the ship's excursion desk sells the same seats with a mark-up and a bus you didn't need. Book direct weeks ahead for anything with limited seats, and check the port's arrival time against the departure you pick — with an hour's margin.
Check the webcam before you pay for the view
Dalsnibba, the Loen Skylift, Fløyen, Ulriken and Stegastein all have live webcams; the mountain can be in cloud while the pier is sunny. Look before you buy the bus or the lift, and if the top is grey, do the fjord boat or the museum first and go up later — weather in the fjords changes in an hour.
