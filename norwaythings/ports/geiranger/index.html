Geiranger cruise port guide

Norway · from NorwayThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Geiranger tender quay / Seawalk pier

Most ships anchor and tender; queues peak 9–10 AM and just before the last tender back. Buses to the viewpoints leave from the quay.

Typically 8 AM – 5 PM

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Geiranger Fjordservice

    1 min walk · Fjord Cruises & Boat Trips

    The 90-minute fjord sightseeing boat from the quay: under the Seven Sisters and the Suitor, past the abandoned cliff farms with the guide's stories of the ladders that were pulled up when the tax man came.

  2. Geiranger Sjokolade

    2 min walk · Shopping & Souvenirs

    A chocolate maker in the village, with fjord-themed bars, a café and a view.

  3. Brasserie Posten

    2 min walk · Restaurants

    The waterfront restaurant in the old post office by the quay: fjord trout, deer, burgers and local beer on a terrace over the water, with the ships in front of you.

  4. Geiranger Church

    4 min walk · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    The white octagonal church of 1842 on the hillside above the quay, with a painted ceiling and a graveyard looking down the fjord to the ships.

  5. Westerås Gard

    11 min walk · Restaurants

    A working mountain farm 300 metres above the fjord, with a restaurant serving its own lamb and elk on a terrace over the water, and the trailheads for Storseterfossen and the Løsta viewpoint.

  6. Norwegian Fjord Centre

    11 min walk · Museums & Culture

    Norsk Fjordsenter, a ten-minute walk uphill from the quay: how the fjord was formed, how people farmed the cliff ledges, the avalanches, the UNESCO listing, and a film.

  7. Storseterfossen

    24 min walk · Hikes, Glaciers & Rides

    The waterfall you walk behind: a 1.5-hour round-trip hike from Westerås farm up a steep path to a ledge that passes under the falling water.

  8. Flydalsjuvet

    24 min walk · Waterfalls & Viewpoints

    The classic viewpoint above the village: the fjord, the ship and the mountains from a rock ledge 4 km up the Stryn road — the photo on every brochure.

  9. Ørnesvingen

    60 min walk · Waterfalls & Viewpoints

    The Eagle Bend: the top hairpin of the Eagle Road on the north side of the fjord, 620 metres up, with a viewing platform over the fjord, the Seven Sisters and the ships.

  10. Seven Sisters Waterfall

    5 mi / 8 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Waterfalls & Viewpoints

    De syv søstrene: seven streams falling 250 metres side by side into the Geirangerfjord, with the Suitor falling opposite and the Bridal Veil next to it — the postcard of the fjord, seen from the water.

  11. Dalsnibba Skywalk

    5 mi / 9 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Waterfalls & Viewpoints

    A glass-floored platform at 1,500 metres, at the end of a toll road 21 km from the village: the whole Geirangerfjord 1,500 metres below, snow in July and the highest fjord view in Europe by road.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Ship time, tender time, and the last boat back
All-aboard is in ship's time, which is not always local time — check the daily programme. In Geiranger the last tender back runs 30–60 minutes before all-aboard and the queue is longest right then; in Flåm the train and the fjord boat both run late on two-ship days. Be at the gangway 30 minutes early. The ship waits for its own excursions, not for you.
Book the operators direct, not the ship's desk
The Flåm Railway, Nærøyfjord boat, Loen Skylift, Fløibanen, Lysefjord and Geiranger cruises all sell online at fixed prices; the ship's excursion desk sells the same seats with a mark-up and a bus you didn't need. Book direct weeks ahead for anything with limited seats, and check the port's arrival time against the departure you pick — with an hour's margin.
Check the webcam before you pay for the view
Dalsnibba, the Loen Skylift, Fløyen, Ulriken and Stegastein all have live webcams; the mountain can be in cloud while the pier is sunny. Look before you buy the bus or the lift, and if the top is grey, do the fjord boat or the museum first and go up later — weather in the fjords changes in an hour.
