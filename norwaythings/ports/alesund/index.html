Ålesund cruise port guide

Norway · from NorwayThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Storneskaia / Skansekaia

Both quays are in the centre: the Art Nouveau streets start at the gangway and the Aksla steps are ten minutes away.

Typically 8 AM – 6 PM

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Fisheries Museum

    1 min walk · Museums & Culture

    The Fiskerimuseet in an 1861 warehouse on the harbour: the klippfisk (dried salt cod) trade that built Ålesund, the boats, the cod-liver oil, and the smell of it.

  2. Brosundet

    3 min walk · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    The canal between the town's two islands, lined with Art Nouveau facades and fishing boats — the heart of Ålesund and the view from every café.

  3. Apotekergata No. 5

    4 min walk · Restaurants

    The restaurant of the Hotel Brosundet, in a fish warehouse on the canal: bacalao, langoustines and the day's catch from the boats you can see out of the window, cooked by one of the best kitchens in Norway.

  4. XL Diner

    4 min walk · Restaurants

    The bacalao restaurant: Ålesund's salt cod cooked a dozen ways in a first-floor room over the canal, from the Portuguese classic to the local tomato stew.

  5. Invit Espressobar

    5 min walk · Cafés & Bakeries

    The coffee bar on Apotekergata, in an Art Nouveau building on the canal, with a pontoon deck on the water: proper espresso, cinnamon buns, and the best seat in town for the price of a coffee.

  6. Art Nouveau Town

    6 min walk · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    Ålesund burned to the ground in 1904 and was rebuilt in three years in Jugendstil: 320 stone buildings with turrets, dragons, flowers and Norse motifs, on two islands around a canal.

  7. Jugendstilsenteret & KUBE

    6 min walk · Museums & Culture

    The Art Nouveau Centre in the old Swan Pharmacy (1907), the finest interior in town, with a time machine film about the 1904 fire and the rebuilding; KUBE next door is the contemporary art museum.

  8. Ålesund Church

    7 min walk · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    The 1909 stone church with the tall tower on the hill in the centre, its interior covered in frescoes and stained glass from the 1920s.

  9. Molo Brew

    9 min walk · Bars & Nightlife

    A brewpub on the pier at the harbour mouth, in a former fish warehouse, with its own beers, pizza and the ships coming and going outside.

  10. Byparken & Rollo

    10 min walk · Parks & Nature

    The town park at the foot of Aksla, with the statue of Rollo — the Viking from here who founded Normandy — given by the city of Rouen in 1911, and the start of the 418 steps.

  11. Aksla Viewpoint

    16 min walk · Waterfalls & Viewpoints

    The town mountain: 418 steps from the town park to Fjellstua, the viewpoint café at 189 metres, with the whole Art Nouveau town on its islands below, the Sunnmøre Alps behind and your ship in the middle.

  12. Atlantic Sea Park

    50 min walk · Family Fun

    Atlanterhavsparken, one of Europe's largest saltwater aquariums, built into the coastal rocks 3 km west of town: the big Atlantic tank fed by divers, seals, penguins, otters, and rock pools to touch.

  13. Sunnmøre Museum

    4 mi / 6 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Museums & Culture

    An open-air museum of 55 old houses and boats from the district on a hill by the medieval town site of Borgund, with replica Viking ships, a medieval museum and paths through the woods.

  14. Alnes Lighthouse

    8 mi / 12 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    A red lighthouse on the open Atlantic at the fishing village of Alnes on Godøy island, 20 km west through undersea tunnels: a gallery and café in the lighthouse, a beach, and nothing between you and Greenland.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Ship time, tender time, and the last boat back
All-aboard is in ship's time, which is not always local time — check the daily programme. In Geiranger the last tender back runs 30–60 minutes before all-aboard and the queue is longest right then; in Flåm the train and the fjord boat both run late on two-ship days. Be at the gangway 30 minutes early. The ship waits for its own excursions, not for you.
Book the operators direct, not the ship's desk
The Flåm Railway, Nærøyfjord boat, Loen Skylift, Fløibanen, Lysefjord and Geiranger cruises all sell online at fixed prices; the ship's excursion desk sells the same seats with a mark-up and a bus you didn't need. Book direct weeks ahead for anything with limited seats, and check the port's arrival time against the departure you pick — with an hour's margin.
Check the webcam before you pay for the view
Dalsnibba, the Loen Skylift, Fløyen, Ulriken and Stegastein all have live webcams; the mountain can be in cloud while the pier is sunny. Look before you buy the bus or the lift, and if the top is grey, do the fjord boat or the museum first and go up later — weather in the fjords changes in an hour.
