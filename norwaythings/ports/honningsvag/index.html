Honningsvåg cruise port guide

Norway · from NorwayThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Honningsvåg harbour quay (tenders when two ships call)

The North Cape is 34 km away: buses and excursions leave from the quay, three and a half hours round trip. The town itself takes half an hour.

Typically 8 AM – 5 PM

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Nordkappmuseet

    2 min walk · Museums & Culture

    The small museum in Honningsvåg's tourist office building: the history of the North Cape tourism since 1664, the fishing town and the 1944 scorched-earth evacuation of Finnmark that left only the church standing.

  2. King Crab Safari, Honningsvåg

    3 min walk · Kayak, RIB & Fjord Safaris

    The Barents Sea king crab — introduced from Russia in the 1960s, now the local fortune — hauled up from the pots on a RIB trip out of the harbour and cooked on the shore afterwards.

  3. Honningsvåg Church

    9 min walk · Landmarks & Stave Churches

    The 1885 wooden church, the only building in Honningsvåg the retreating German army did not burn in 1944, and where the returning townspeople lived through the winter of 1945.

  4. North Cape (Nordkapp)

    18 mi / 29 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Waterfalls & Viewpoints

    The 307-metre cliff at 71°10' N that stands in for the top of Europe: the globe monument, the visitor centre cut into the rock with a panoramic film and a chapel, and the Barents Sea straight ahead to the pole.

  5. Knivskjellodden

    20 mi / 32 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Hikes, Glaciers & Rides

    The real northernmost point of mainland Europe, 1.5 km further north than the Cape and only reachable on foot: an 18 km round-trip hike from the road across the tundra to a cairn at sea level, with the North Cape cliff opposite.

  6. Gjesværstappan Bird Cliffs

    21 mi / 34 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Kayak, RIB & Fjord Safaris

    The three bird islands off Gjesvær, 30 km from town: a million seabirds — puffins, gannets, sea eagles, kittiwakes — from May to August, reached by a 90-minute boat safari from the village.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Ship time, tender time, and the last boat back
All-aboard is in ship's time, which is not always local time — check the daily programme. In Geiranger the last tender back runs 30–60 minutes before all-aboard and the queue is longest right then; in Flåm the train and the fjord boat both run late on two-ship days. Be at the gangway 30 minutes early. The ship waits for its own excursions, not for you.
Book the operators direct, not the ship's desk
The Flåm Railway, Nærøyfjord boat, Loen Skylift, Fløibanen, Lysefjord and Geiranger cruises all sell online at fixed prices; the ship's excursion desk sells the same seats with a mark-up and a bus you didn't need. Book direct weeks ahead for anything with limited seats, and check the port's arrival time against the departure you pick — with an hour's margin.
Check the webcam before you pay for the view
Dalsnibba, the Loen Skylift, Fløyen, Ulriken and Stegastein all have live webcams; the mountain can be in cloud while the pier is sunny. Look before you buy the bus or the lift, and if the top is grey, do the fjord boat or the museum first and go up later — weather in the fjords changes in an hour.
