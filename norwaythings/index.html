NorwayThings

Nineteen ports. Every one a race against all-aboard.

The Norwegian fjords are the most beautiful cruise itinerary on earth, and every port day is a race against the clock. Bergen, Stavanger and the Lysefjord, Haugesund, Flåm, Eidfjord, Olden, Geiranger, Ålesund, Åndalsnes, Molde, Oslo, Kristiansand, Trondheim, Bodø, Lofoten, Tromsø, Alta, Honningsvåg and the North Cape, Kirkenes — nineteen ports in one app.

Built for a port day

For each port: where the ship actually ties up, how many minutes every place is from the pier, and a ready-made day timed to get you back before all-aboard.

What needs booking before you sail

The Flåm Railway, the Loen Skylift, the North Cape bus, the Trollfjord boat — the app tells you which experiences sell out and links you straight to the operator's own booking page.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time — it all works in airplane mode.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
