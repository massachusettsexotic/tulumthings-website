RoatanThings

West Bay is farther than it looks.

Ships tie up at Mahogany Bay or the Town Center pier in Coxen Hole, and the famous stuff is on the other end of the island. RoatanThings covers the whole of Roatán: West Bay and West End, the barrier reef, Gumbalimba, Carambola, the Keys and the quiet east end.

Taxis and travel times, honestly

How long the taxi to West Bay really takes from each pier, what to agree on before you get in, and which days only fit a long port call. Safety notes quoted from the US State Department, not guessed.

Off the cruise pier

Every place shows minutes from your pier, named, with an all-aboard reminder and seventeen ready-made days timed around the ship.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time, so the guide works in airplane mode. Only the map background needs a signal.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
