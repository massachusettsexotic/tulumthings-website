AmsterdamThings

Amsterdam is small, dense and easy to do wrong.

Canal-ring wandering, brown cafes, the Jordaan, markets, windmills you can actually reach — and the Rijksmuseum and Van Gogh with honest notes about when to book ahead. Hand-written descriptions for the famous places; OpenStreetMap keeps the rest complete.

Live opening hours

Every listing carries real hours with "open now" status — find out the market is closed before crossing town, not after. Canal cruises and tours link straight to the operator's own booking page.

Works without a roaming plan. Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
