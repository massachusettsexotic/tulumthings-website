AlaskaThings

Your port day is short. Make every hour count.

A port-day guide to the Alaska cruise circuit — Ketchikan, Juneau, Skagway, Sitka, Icy Strait Point, Haines, Klawock, Wrangell, Petersburg, Seward, Whittier, Valdez, Kodiak, Anchorage, and Victoria BC — with 5,700+ real places, every one showing how far it is from the pier on foot.

Book direct, skip the markup

Whale watching, flightseeing, glacier hikes: the excursion operators in AlaskaThings are real local companies with verified websites and phone numbers. The app links travelers straight to the operator's own booking page — many run on FareHarbor and other booking platforms — so the traveler pays the operator's own price and the money stays in port.

Works offline, for real

Every description, opening time and NOAA tide table ships inside the app, and free Offline Port Packs download every photo ahead of time — the whole port works in airplane mode at the pier.

Port Day Mode

Set your all-aboard time and every place shows minutes from the pier, so you always know what still fits.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
