Skagway cruise port guide

Alaska · from AlaskaThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Broadway / Railroad / Ore docks

Three docks; Broadway is a 5-minute walk to town, the Railroad Dock ~12 and the Ore Dock ~15 (free SMART shuttle from the far docks). The train boards at the Railroad Dock or the depot in town.

Typically 7 AM – 8 PM

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Red Onion Saloon

    12 min walk · Bars & Saloons

    Skagway's 1898 brothel-turned-saloon on Broadway — a bar downstairs with good food and beer, and a cheeky 'brothel museum' tour upstairs given by the 'madams'.

  2. Yakutania Point

    14 min walk · Parks & Nature

    A rocky point across the footbridge west of town with a picnic shelter and views down the fjord to your ship — an easy 30-minute walk from the pier.

  3. Skagway Brewing Company

    15 min walk · Restaurants & Seafood

    The town brewery on 7th and Broadway — Spruce Tip Blonde, halibut and chips, and a rooftop deck when the sun's out.

  4. Klondike Gold Rush Visitor Center

    16 min walk · Museums & Galleries

    The National Park Service center for the gold-rush town — Skagway's downtown is the park.

  5. White Pass & Yukon Route Railroad

    19 min walk · Excursions & Tours

    The 1898 narrow-gauge railway climbing 2,865 feet from the pier to the White Pass summit through tunnels, trestles and the Dead Horse Gulch of the gold rush.

  6. Skagway Museum

    19 min walk · Museums & Galleries

    The town museum in the granite McCabe Building — gold-rush relics, a Tlingit canoe, and Soapy Smith's story, five minutes from the docks.

  7. Lower Dewey Lake Trail

    20 min walk · Hiking & Trails

    A 20-minute switchback climb from the edge of town to a quiet mountain lake with a 2-mi loop around it — the best short hike from any pier in Alaska.

  8. Jewell Gardens

    46 min walk · Parks & Nature

    Show gardens and a glassblowing studio on the site of a gold-rush dairy farm 1.5 mi from town — flowers, a G-scale railway, and glassblowing demonstrations.

  9. Gold Rush Cemetery & Reid Falls

    49 min walk · Landmarks & History

    Where Soapy Smith and Frank Reid lie after their 1898 shoot-out, with a short trail beyond to a 300-foot waterfall.

  10. Dyea

    5 mi / 9 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Landmarks & History

    The ghost town at the foot of the Chilkoot Trail, 9 mi from Skagway — a few false fronts in a meadow, an 1898 cemetery, and tidal flats full of birds and bears.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Book direct, not through the ship
The same whale boat, train or floatplane typically costs 30–60% less on the operator's own website than in the ship's excursion catalog. The ship's price buys one thing: the guarantee it will wait. In port towns this small, that's rarely worth the difference.
Check whose clock you're on
Ships often stay on Pacific time while Alaska runs an hour behind. Your phone switches; the ship's clocks don't. Set a manual alarm in ship time for all-aboard.
Ask for the return guarantee
Most Juneau whale-watch and glacier operators, and the White Pass railroad, promise to have you back before all-aboard or cover your way to the next port. If the operator offers it, the ship's markup buys nothing.
The jewelry strip is not local
The same chains fill the storefronts by the dock in every port and pay the cruise lines for the 'recommended store' map. Buy Native art at the heritage centers with the Silver Hand seal, and anything else a block or two back from the pier.
