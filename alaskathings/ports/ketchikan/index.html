Ketchikan cruise port guide

Alaska · from AlaskaThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Downtown Berths 1–4

Berths 1–4 are on Front Street — you step off into downtown. Ward Cove (Berth 5, Norwegian ships) is 7 mi north with a free shuttle, ~20 min.

Typically 7 AM – 3 PM

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Southeast Alaska Discovery Center

    4 min walk · Museums & Galleries

    The Forest Service's visitor center on the waterfront — rainforest, native culture and fishing exhibits, a theater, and rangers who know every trail in the Tongass.

  2. Creek Street

    5 min walk · Landmarks & History

    Ketchikan's famous boardwalk on pilings over Ketchikan Creek — the old red-light district, now galleries, shops and Dolly's House museum.

  3. Great Alaskan Lumberjack Show

    6 min walk · Family Fun

    An hour of axe-throwing, log-rolling and speed-climbing in a covered arena a block from the docks — corny, loud, and a hit with kids.

  4. Alaska Fish House

    7 min walk · Restaurants & Seafood

    Waterfront fish shack by the docks — fish and chips, salmon, crab, and a deck over the harbor.

  5. Dolly's House

    7 min walk · Museums & Galleries

    The preserved 1919 home and business of Dolly Arthur, Creek Street's best-known madam — kept as she left it, with the tour guides in period dress.

  6. Totem Heritage Center

    14 min walk · Totems & Native Culture

    The largest collection of original 19th-century totem poles in the world, rescued from abandoned Tlingit and Haida villages — unrestored, and all the more powerful for it.

  7. Rainbird Trail

    17 min walk · Hiking & Trails

    A 1.3-mi forest trail on the hill above downtown with views over the channel — the easy answer to 'a walk in the rainforest' within reach of the ship.

  8. Deer Mountain Trail

    21 min walk · Hiking & Trails

    The town's mountain — a steep 2.5-mi climb from the edge of downtown to a viewpoint over the channel and the ships, with the first overlook an hour up.

  9. Misty Fjords Flightseeing

    3 mi / 5 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Excursions & Tours

    Floatplane flights over the Misty Fjords National Monument — 3,000-foot granite walls, waterfalls and lakes, with a landing on a mountain lake.

  10. Saxman Native Village

    3 mi / 5 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Totems & Native Culture

    Tlingit village 2.5 mi south of town with the largest standing collection of totem poles anywhere, a clan house, and a carving shed where poles are still made.

  11. Totem Bight State Historical Park

    9 mi / 15 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Totems & Native Culture

    A recreated Tlingit clan house and fourteen totem poles in the rainforest on the shore 10 mi north of town — quieter and more atmospheric than Saxman.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Book direct, not through the ship
The same whale boat, train or floatplane typically costs 30–60% less on the operator's own website than in the ship's excursion catalog. The ship's price buys one thing: the guarantee it will wait. In port towns this small, that's rarely worth the difference.
Check whose clock you're on
Ships often stay on Pacific time while Alaska runs an hour behind. Your phone switches; the ship's clocks don't. Set a manual alarm in ship time for all-aboard.
Ask for the return guarantee
Most Juneau whale-watch and glacier operators, and the White Pass railroad, promise to have you back before all-aboard or cover your way to the next port. If the operator offers it, the ship's markup buys nothing.
The jewelry strip is not local
The same chains fill the storefronts by the dock in every port and pay the cruise lines for the 'recommended store' map. Buy Native art at the heritage centers with the Silver Hand seal, and anything else a block or two back from the pier.
