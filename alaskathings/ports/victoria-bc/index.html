British Columbia, Canada · from AlaskaThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Ogden Point

Usually an evening call (about 5–11 PM). The Inner Harbour is a 25-minute walk along the waterfront, or $15 by shuttle/taxi. You are in Canada — passport rules and phone roaming apply.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Fisherman's Wharf

    16 min walk · Restaurants & Seafood

    Floating houses, fish-and-chip barges and harbor seals begging for scraps, ten minutes from the cruise pier on the way to the Inner Harbour.

  2. Royal BC Museum

    25 min walk · Museums & Galleries

    British Columbia's provincial museum on the Inner Harbour — a walk-through Old Town, a First Nations gallery with a full longhouse, natural history dioramas, and an IMAX.

  3. Beacon Hill Park

    29 min walk · Parks & Nature

    A 200-acre park between the cruise pier and downtown — peacocks, a petting farm, the world's tallest free-standing totem pole, and cliffs over the Strait with the Olympic Mountains beyond.

  4. Inner Harbour

    31 min walk · Landmarks & History

    Victoria's postcard — the ivy-covered Fairmont Empress, the illuminated Parliament Buildings, buskers on the causeway, floatplanes landing, and water taxis.

  5. Fan Tan Alley & Chinatown

    36 min walk · Landmarks & History

    Canada's oldest Chinatown, with the narrowest street in the country — a shoulder-wide alley of tiny shops between Fisgard and Pandora.

  6. Craigdarroch Castle

    57 min walk · Landmarks & History

    A coal baron's 1890 Victorian mansion with 39 rooms of stained glass and woodwork on a hill above town — Victoria's most-visited historic house.

  7. Butchart Gardens

    14 mi / 23 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Parks & Nature

    Fifty-five acres of show gardens in a former limestone quarry 14 mi north of Victoria — the Sunken Garden, the Rose Garden, the Japanese Garden, and Saturday-night fireworks in summer.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Book direct, not through the ship
The same whale boat, train or floatplane typically costs 30–60% less on the operator's own website than in the ship's excursion catalog. The ship's price buys one thing: the guarantee it will wait. In port towns this small, that's rarely worth the difference.
Check whose clock you're on
Ships often stay on Pacific time while Alaska runs an hour behind. Your phone switches; the ship's clocks don't. Set a manual alarm in ship time for all-aboard.
Ask for the return guarantee
Most Juneau whale-watch and glacier operators, and the White Pass railroad, promise to have you back before all-aboard or cover your way to the next port. If the operator offers it, the ship's markup buys nothing.
The jewelry strip is not local
The same chains fill the storefronts by the dock in every port and pay the cruise lines for the 'recommended store' map. Buy Native art at the heritage centers with the Silver Hand seal, and anything else a block or two back from the pier.
