MaltaThings

Seventeen miles long. Seven thousand years deep.

Malta is 17 miles long and has 7,000 years of history on it — older than the pyramids, and most of it walkable. MaltaThings covers Valletta and the Three Cities, Mdina and the centre, the north and the ferry to Gozo, and Comino's Blue Lagoon.

Which bus, which boat

Malta runs on buses and ferries, and knowing which one is most of the battle. Fares, routes and the crossings to Gozo and Comino, with real prices checked against the operators.

Off the cruise gangway

Arriving at the Grand Harbour? Every place shows how far it is on foot from where you step off, with an all-aboard reminder.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time — it all works in airplane mode.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
