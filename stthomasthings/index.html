StThomasThings

Two piers, two islands, one day.

Ships tie up at Havensight or Crown Bay, and the best of the Virgin Islands is spread across St. Thomas and St. John. StThomasThings covers both: Charlotte Amalie's forts and steps, Magens Bay, Coki Point, and the ferry to St. John's national-park beaches.

Safari taxis and ferries, explained

How the open-air taxis charge, when the Red Hook ferry runs, and how long each beach really takes from your pier. US dollars, US phones, driving on the left.

Off the cruise pier

Every place shows minutes from whichever pier your ship uses, with an all-aboard reminder and six ready-made days timed around the ship.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time, so the guide works in airplane mode. Only the map background needs a signal.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
