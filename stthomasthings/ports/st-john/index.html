St. John cruise day guide

US Virgin Islands, by ferry from St. Thomas · from StThomasThings, the offline iPhone guide

Getting there from the ship

Ferries to Cruz Bay leave Red Hook, on the East End, about every hour — 30–40 minutes by taxi from the piers. The Charlotte Amalie–Cruz Bay ferry was suspended through October 2026. Plan to be back on St. Thomas at least two hours before all-aboard.

The best day on St. John starts on the first ferry you can reach from Red Hook. Coming back, allow the 20-minute crossing, a wait for the next boat, and 30–40 minutes of taxi to the pier — so catch a ferry at least two hours before all-aboard.

Top places near the Cruz Bay ferry dock

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Woody's Seafood Saloon

    3 min walk · Bars & Nightlife

    The Cruz Bay bar that spills onto the street at happy hour, with seafood, sandwiches and cold beer a minute from the ferry.

  2. Mongoose Junction

    7 min walk · Shopping & Souvenirs

    A shopping courtyard in Cruz Bay built in stone and wood among the trees — jewellers, galleries and cafés.

  3. Honeymoon Beach (St. John)

    20 min walk · Beaches

    A calm beach inside the national park, reached by the Lind Point Trail from Cruz Bay — clear water, snorkelling on the reef and watersports rentals.

  4. Hawksnest Beach

    45 min walk · Beaches

    The first North Shore beach from Cruz Bay: a short, pretty curve of sand with shade trees and a reef off the point.

  5. Peace Hill

    51 min walk · Forts & Landmarks

    A short climb from the North Shore Road to a hilltop with ruins of a sugar mill and the remains of the 'Christ of the Caribbean' statue, looking down on Hawksnest and Trunk Bay.

  6. Trunk Bay

    3 mi / 5 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Beaches

    The postcard beach of the Virgin Islands National Park: white sand, turquoise water and an underwater snorkelling trail with plaques on the reef around Trunk Cay.

  7. Reef Bay Trail

    4 mi / 7 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Trails & Tours

    The park's classic hike: down through forest to Taíno petroglyphs carved beside a freshwater pool, and the ruins of a sugar mill by the sea.

  8. Cinnamon Bay

    4 mi / 7 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Beaches

    The longest beach on the North Shore, with a campground, a watersports rental, and a short loop trail through the ruins of a sugar estate.

  9. Maho Bay

    5 mi / 8 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Snorkel & Dive

    A shallow, sandy bay right along the North Shore Road where green turtles graze the sea grass a few metres from shore.

  10. Francis Bay

    5 mi / 9 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Beaches

    A quiet beach beyond Maho with a salt pond behind it where herons and other birds gather, and shade under the sea grapes.

  11. Annaberg Sugar Plantation

    6 mi / 10 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Forts & Landmarks

    The best-preserved sugar estate ruins in the park: the windmill tower, boiling house and slave quarters, on a hill looking across to Tortola and Jost Van Dyke.

  12. Waterlemon Cay

    7 mi / 11 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Snorkel & Dive

    A small cay in Leinster Bay reached by a 20-minute shoreline walk and a swim — one of the best snorkels on St. John, with turtles, rays and coral.

  13. Emmaus Moravian Church

    7 mi / 12 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Forts & Landmarks

    The Moravian church on the hill above Coral Bay, founded in the 18th century — the landmark of the island's quiet east end.

  14. Skinny Legs

    7 mi / 12 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Restaurants

    The open-air bar and grill in Coral Bay, a gathering place for boaters and locals — burgers, cold beer, and a laid-back afternoon.

  15. Salt Pond Bay

    8 mi / 13 km from the Cruz Bay ferry dock — taxi or shuttle · Beaches

    A calm, sandy bay on the south-east of St. John, a short walk from the road, with good snorkelling on the rocks.

Getting around: what the app tells you

Ask the fare, and count heads
Taxi fares are per person and per destination, set by the VI Taxicab Commission, and every taxi should carry the rate sheet. Say the destination, ask 'how much per person?', and agree before you sit down. Share a safari with other passengers going the same way and it's the cheapest ride on the island.
St. John: go early, come back early
The best day on St. John starts on the first ferry you can reach from Red Hook. Coming back, allow the 20-minute crossing, a wait for the next boat, and 30–40 minutes of taxi to the pier — so catch a ferry at least two hours before all-aboard.
Set an alarm on ship time
Island clocks are Atlantic Standard Time, all year. Ships often keep a different time. Before you go ashore, check the time on the ship's app or a clock on board and set your phone alarm by that — the ship will not wait.
