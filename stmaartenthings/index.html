StMaartenThings

Two countries, one port day.

Ships tie up at Philipsburg, and the island is two countries: Dutch Sint Maarten and French Saint-Martin, with no border control between them. StMaartenThings covers both sides: Great Bay and Front Street, the planes at Maho, Orient Bay and Pinel Island, Marigot market and Grand Case.

Taxis, water taxis and ferries, explained

What a taxi should cost to each beach, the water taxi from the pier to town, the buses, and the ferries to Anguilla and St. Barths (bring a passport).

Off the cruise pier

Every place shows minutes from the pier, with an all-aboard reminder and nine ready-made days that get you back to the ship on time.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time, so the guide works in airplane mode. Only the map background needs a signal.

Eleven languages. No account, no ads, no tracking.
