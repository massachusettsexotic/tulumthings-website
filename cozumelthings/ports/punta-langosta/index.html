Punta Langosta cruise port guide

Cozumel, Mexico · from CozumelThings, the offline iPhone guide

Where ships dock

Punta Langosta pier, downtown San Miguel

The downtown pier: you walk off through the Punta Langosta mall onto the waterfront. The main square and the ferry to Playa del Carmen are about ten minutes' walk north. Taxis for everywhere else wait at the stand by the mall; the tariff is posted there.

Typically 7–8 AM to 4–6 PM · Cozumel is UTC−5 all year — check which clock your ship keeps

All-aboard is in ship time. Check the daily programme and be back at the gangway 30 minutes early.

Top places near the pier

Walking times come from the app: straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at an easy 4.5 km/h. Places further than an hour on foot show the distance instead.

  1. Malecón (Avenida Rafael Melgar)

    8 min walk · Ruins, Lighthouses & Landmarks

    The seafront boulevard that runs the length of downtown, from Punta Langosta past the ferry pier and the main square to the museum and beyond.

  2. La Choza

    10 min walk · Restaurants & Taquerías

    A family restaurant a block inland from the waterfront serving Yucatecan and Mexican home cooking — cochinita pibil, pollo en relleno negro, fresh tortillas — under a big palapa roof.

  3. Parque Benito Juárez

    12 min walk · Ruins, Lighthouses & Landmarks

    San Miguel's main square, a block back from the ferry pier: a shaded plaza with the clock tower, benches, snack carts and, on Sunday evenings, local families, music and dancing.

  4. Iglesia San Miguel

    14 min walk · Ruins, Lighthouses & Landmarks

    The island's main Catholic church, a block from the main square.

  5. Mercado Municipal

    14 min walk · Markets & Food Stalls

    San Miguel's municipal market, a few blocks inland: fruit, meat and fish stalls and, at the back, the cocinas económicas — little kitchens serving cheap home-style lunches to people who work in town.

  6. Wet Wendy's Margarita House

    15 min walk · Bars & Nightlife

    A lively downtown bar-restaurant known for its long margarita list and Mexican dishes, a short walk from the main square.

  7. Kinta

    16 min walk · Restaurants & Taquerías

    A downtown restaurant serving modern Mexican cooking in a courtyard garden a few blocks back from the waterfront — one of the town's well-known dinner spots.

  8. Museo de la Isla de Cozumel

    18 min walk · Museums & Galleries

    The island museum in a former hotel on the waterfront boulevard: rooms on Maya Cozumel and the sacred pilgrimage to Ixchel, the coral reefs and their creatures, pirates, chicle and the fishing town that followed.

  9. Guido's

    19 min walk · Restaurants & Taquerías

    A long-running Italian restaurant on the waterfront boulevard north of the main square, known for wood-oven pizza and home-made pasta.

  10. Playa Caletita

    44 min walk · Beaches & Beach Clubs

    A small public beach just north of the International Pier, on the coast road: a patch of sand, a few palapas and calm, clear water.

  11. Cozumel Country Club

    4 mi / 7 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Spas & Golf

    The island's 18-hole golf course, laid out among mangroves and lagoons north of town.

  12. Playa San Juan

    5 mi / 8 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Beaches & Beach Clubs

    A strip of sand and shallow water on the hotel strip north of town, fronting the northern hotels and a couple of beach clubs.

  13. San Gervasio

    9 mi / 15 km from the pier — taxi or shuttle · Ruins, Lighthouses & Landmarks

    The largest Maya site on Cozumel, in the forest in the middle of the island: plazas, small temples, the arch and the sacbé roads of the sanctuary to Ixchel, goddess of fertility and the moon, which drew pilgrims from the mainland before…

Getting around: what the app tells you

Ask the tariff, not the driver
Every pier's taxi stand posts the union tariff by destination. Read it (or photograph it) before you get in, and agree the fare for the return too. It's per car for up to four people, so sharing with people from your ship halves the cost.
Carry some pesos
Dollars work, but a few hundred pesos from a bank ATM make tacos, markets and in-town taxis cheaper and simpler. Keep small bills; many small shops can't change a 500-peso note.
Island time, ship time
Cozumel doesn't change its clocks. Set your phone to the ship's all-aboard time and add a margin: taxis from the far beaches take 30–45 minutes, and the pier gates close before the ship sails.
Playa del Carmen is a gamble on a short day
The ferry takes about 45 minutes each way, plus queues, plus the taxi between the ferry pier and a southern cruise pier. With less than seven hours ashore, Cozumel's own beaches and reefs are the better day.
