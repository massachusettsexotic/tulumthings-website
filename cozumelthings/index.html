Three piers, one island day.

Ships tie up at Punta Langosta downtown or at the International Pier and Puerta Maya south of town, and the island's best water is spread along the west coast. CozumelThings covers the reefs, Chankanaab, the beach clubs, Punta Sur, the wild east side, San Gervasio and downtown San Miguel.

Taxis, reefs and the ferry, explained

What a taxi should cost from each pier, which reefs you can reach from shore and which need a boat, and why the ferry to Playa del Carmen needs a hard eye on the all-aboard time.

Off the cruise pier

Every place shows minutes from your pier, with an all-aboard reminder and eight ready-made days timed around the ship.

Works offline, for real

Every description and opening time ships inside the app, and the photos download ahead of time, so the guide works in airplane mode. Only the map background needs a signal.
